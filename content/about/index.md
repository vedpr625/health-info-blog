I'm a health informatics architect currently working on a country-level UNOPS health interoperability project in Sri Lanka, with prior experience in India and Mongolia.

This blog is where I unpack what it actually looks like to design and build interoperable health information systems — the standards debates, the political and institutional realities, the technical trade-offs, and the lessons that don't make it into the official project documentation.

I write for fellow architects, implementers, and anyone curious about what it takes to make health data move across systems, institutions, and borders.
